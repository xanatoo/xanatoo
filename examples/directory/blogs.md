## How to follow more

Many personal sites expose Atom/RSS at `/feed`, `/atom.xml`, or `/index.xml`.
**Ctrl+L** → `feed:https://their.site/` often finds it. See also
[Feeds](../feeds.md) and [Social](social.md).
