## A note on “top sites”

Commercial top-100 lists are mostly search, social, shopping, and video
front doors. They are fine to open, but they are a poor match for a
document reader. The lists above favour sources that still work as text,
feeds, or archives. For more structured picks see
[News](news.md), [Tech](tech.md), [Science](science.md), and
[Blogs](blogs.md).
