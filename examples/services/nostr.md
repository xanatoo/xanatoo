# Nostr — people, groups and projects

[← Services](../services.md)

[Nostr](https://nostr.com/) is a protocol for publishing signed events to
independent relays. Open an author's public key (`npub1…` or `nprofile1…`)
in xanatoo to see their profile and recent notes. A note ID (`note1…` or
`nevent1…`) opens its thread. **Ctrl+L** accepts those addresses directly.
The **Nostr Explorer** panel shows the relays, authors and groups xanatoo
has seen.

## Live notes and discovery

- [Recent public notes](nostr:recent) — a live sample from public relays.
- [Discover public groups](nostr:groups) — scan known NIP-29 relays and open a group's timeline.
- [Primal Explore](https://primal.net/explore) — trending notes, people and search in a web client.
- [Nostr.Band](https://nostr.band/) — search notes and profiles and explore trends.
- [Jumble](https://jumble.social/) — browse one relay's feed at a time.
- [Nostr.Watch](https://nostr.watch/) — find relays and see their reported status.
- [njump](https://njump.me/) — open a public Nostr address on the web.
- [Following.space](https://following.space/) — browse and share follow packs.

## Public groups and forums

- **Nostr Protocol Forum** — [discover its groups in xanatoo](nostr:groups@relay.nostr-proto.org) or [open the forum](https://nostr-proto.org/). Rooms include [Agora](https://nostr-proto.org/room/agora) for general discussion, [Help](https://nostr-proto.org/room/help), [Protocol](https://nostr-proto.org/room/protocol), [Apps](https://nostr-proto.org/room/apps) and [Relays](https://nostr-proto.org/room/relays).
- **Groups** — [discover groups on its relay](nostr:groups@relay.groups.nip29.com) or [open the web chat](https://groups.nip29.com/).
- **Obelisk** — [discover groups on its public relay](nostr:groups@public.obelisk.ar) or [open Obelisk](https://obelisk.ar/).
- **The fiatjaf pyramid** — [discover groups on the relay](nostr:groups@pyramid.fiatjaf.com) or [browse the group page](https://pyramid.fiatjaf.com/groups/).
- [Flotilla](https://flotilla.social/) · [Chachi](https://github.com/purrgrammer/chachi) · [Nostrord](https://nostrord.com/) — clients for relay-based communities.
- [Nostr Nests](https://nostrnests.com/) — live audio rooms.

Group discovery shows what each relay makes available publicly. Group
membership and private rooms depend on the relay and client.
