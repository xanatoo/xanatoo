# Spartan — hosts

[← Services](../services.md)

[Spartan](spartan://mozz.us/) is an even smaller cousin of Gemini: plain TCP on port 300,
no TLS, gemtext pages, and a one-line request. Uploads (input) go in the request body.
xanatoo opens `spartan://` addresses like Gemini ones.

Spartan has only a handful of servers. These answered on 2026-11-21:

- [spartan.mozz.us](spartan://spartan.mozz.us/) — the protocol's home and specification
- [mozz.us](spartan://mozz.us/) — Michael Lazar's site, the protocol's author
- [Astrobotany](spartan://astrobotany.mozz.us/) — the plant garden, also on Spartan
- [tilde.team](spartan://tilde.team/) — tilde.team's members' pages

Most Spartan content is mirrored on Gemini — see the [Gemini capsules](gemini.md).
