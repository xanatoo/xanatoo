# Gemini — capsules

[← Services](../services.md)

[Gemini](gemini://geminiprotocol.net/) is a small, text-first protocol (port 1965, TLS
always) with its own light markup, *gemtext*. Sites are called *capsules*. xanatoo opens
`gemini://` addresses as ordinary documents; input prompts (search boxes) ask in the
location bar.

## Start here

- [Project Gemini](gemini://geminiprotocol.net/) — the protocol's home, specification and FAQ
- [Kennedy](gemini://kennedy.gemi.dev/) — search engine for Geminispace
- [TLGS](gemini://tlgs.one/) — “Totally Legit Gemini Search”
- [Station](gemini://station.martinrue.com/) — a small Gemini social network
- [BBS](gemini://bbs.geminispace.org/) — Geminispace's bulletin board
- [Midnight Pub](gemini://midnight.pub/) — writing and conversation, a virtual pub
- [Astrobotany](gemini://astrobotany.mozz.us/) — grow a plant together with others
